WHY VENDORED RATHER THAN LINKED. This storefront's defining rule is that the
browser makes no third-party requests — enforced by `script-src 'self'` in the
CSP and asserted as fact in /privacy. A <script src="https://telegram.org/…">
would be blocked by the policy AND would make the privacy page untrue. Serving
it from our own origin keeps both.

INSPECTED BEFORE COMMITTING: it issues no fetch/XHR/WebSocket of its own. The
only URLs in the file are documentation links and the w3.org SVG namespaces. It
talks to the Telegram host through postMessage / TelegramWebviewProxy, which is
the host bridge, not the network.
